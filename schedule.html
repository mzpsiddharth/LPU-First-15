Suggested windows

A low-stress order

  • 4–5 ID card, UMS & LPUTouch. Collect the ID, activate your account and confirm your timetable before classes settle.
  • 6–8 Mess, library & mentor. Sort food, borrow your first book, and meet the person who signs your academic forms.
  • 9–12 Clubs & EDU-Revolution. Try a society, then plan one activity that earns credit beyond the classroom.
  • 13–15 Settle & review. Check attendance, save helplines, and note any reporting form correction before deadlines.
