Phase 1 · Before you travel

Finish it online, sail through the counters

Complete these through LPU Admit → Post Admission Services. The portal steps close many of the queues before you even reach campus.

01

Upload your documents correctly

Log in to the LPUADMIT Portal → Post Admission Services. Upload clear colour scans of originals — never scans of photocopies.

JPEG / PNG / PDF / DOC< 5 MB each
Document upload checklist
  • Check that your address and photograph are accurate — they appear on your ID card.
  • Request an extension via the admission portal if a document is unavailable.
  • Download and save the PDF of your submitted admission form and documents.
  • For a correction: Ask Your Query → Form Data Correction.
  • Carry all originals in a transparent folder for verification.
02

Book hostel & campus facilities

Choose hostel, mess, laundry, transport, gym or sports through the portal. On-campus bookings are possible but subject to availability.

Hostel + messOptional add-ons
Hostel packing list
03

Clear pending fees

Pay any outstanding balance before reporting so the fee counter does not interrupt your loop. Keep the receipt or confirmation.

Scholarship details usually update within 4–5 working days. Still missing? Raise a ticket: Ask Your Query → “Admission Taken But Scholarship Not Updated”.
04

Submit your uniform size

Admission Portal → Post Admission Services → Uniform Size Preference. Submit before you report.

No size submitted means a slower uniform counter. This is the most common avoidable delay on reporting day.
05

Finalise travel & free pick-up

The University runs a 24-hour complimentary pick-up from the nearby railway station and bus stand during reporting and induction. Finalise your arrival plan first.

Railway station Bus stand
Main Gate on the map
06

Reach the campus reporting venue

At the pick-up point, contact the LPU Help Desk. Officials guide you to the designated shuttle and on to Baldev Raj Mittal Unipolis.

Induction & Reporting Helpline: 01824-517170. Save it before you leave home.
Pack the reporting folder: originals + one photocopy of every certificate, anti-ragging affidavit, fee receipt, passport photos, Aadhaar, and a pen. Keep it in a transparent folder on top of your bag.
Phase 2 · On campus

The 8-step Unipolis workflow

Follow the help-desk order. Your Reporting & Induction Sheet must be stamped at every counter before you move on.

Unipolis on the map
Keep your phone charged. The first step needs a QR scan and an OTP sent to your registered mobile number. Keep the LPU Admit login handy too.
01

Report at Unipolis

Scan the QR code at the reporting venue to download your Reporting & Induction Sheet and validate it with the OTP on your registered mobile.

  • Collect the Checklist Document from the help desk.
  • Visit the relevant help desk for each activity.
  • Get your sheet stamped after every activity, before moving on.
  • Complete every step — skipping one can block class attendance.
02

Original certificate verification

Present your original certificates for eligibility verification, and submit any pending documents.

  • Keep the transparent folder open and organised.
  • Confirm your eligibility and pending-document status.
  • Get your Induction Sheet stamped before leaving the counter.
03

Biometrics enrolment

Complete biometrics enrolment. The help desk official will tell you the date and venue for ID card collection.

Note the ID card details on your phone. You will need the card to enter hostels and borrow library books.
04

Book hostel & campus facilities

Only if you did not book before arrival. Choose hostel, mess, laundry, transport, gym or sports, subject to availability.

Mess passLaundryTransportGym / sports
05

Fee clearance

If any dues remain, visit the designated fee counter. Keep the receipt or confirmation ready for verification, then proceed.

Save a digital copy of the receipt in your phone. Do not leave the counter without confirmation.
06

Uniform collection

Visit the Uniform Distribution Counter listed on your checklist. Give the Secret Key — sent via UMS My Messages and SMS — only after receiving your uniform.

  • Confirm the uniform matches your submitted measurements.
  • Get the Reporting & Induction Sheet stamped.
07

Activate UMS

Install the LPUTouch app from the App Store or Google Play. After document verification, collect your Verification ID and activate UMS.

Keep handy

The reporting-day kit

  • Transparent document folder
  • Originals + one photocopy set
  • Anti-ragging affidavit
  • Three passport photos
  • Fee receipt / confirmation
  • Charged phone with LPU Admit login
  • Registered mobile for OTP and SMS
  • Uniform Secret Key (UMS + SMS)
  • A pen and a small notebook
  • Water bottle and light snack
Avoid the queue

Three delays you can prevent

  1. 1No uniform size on the portal. Submit it before you travel; the counter cannot guess your size.
  2. 2Scanned photocopies instead of originals. Re-upload clear colour scans or verification stops at Step 2.
  3. 3Unpaid dues. Clear them online so the fee counter is a stamp, not a detour.
Stuck at a counter?

Call the induction desk before you leave the queue.

Document issues, fee receipts, hostel allocation, uniforms or UMS activation — the reporting helpline is staffed through the induction window.
