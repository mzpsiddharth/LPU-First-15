What to bring

The pre-arrival packing pack

Pack once, tick it off, and arrive with the right documents in a transparent folder. Your progress is saved on this device.

Carry originals plus one photocopy of every document. Keep them in a transparent folder for quick verification at Unipolis.

Required documents

0 of 10 complete
Additional certificates (if applicable)
  • Defence document for scholarship or eligibility
  • Domicile certificate (JK migrant, North-East applicants)
  • Gap certificate
  • Interview result (MBA and M.A. English)
  • Medical certificate (Physical Education programmes)
  • Migration certificate (part-time programmes)
  • No objection certificate (part-time Ph.D.)
  • PCI undertaking (M.Pharm.)
  • Reserved category certificate

International students: passport, visa (with issue and expiry dates), FRRO or police verification, health insurance, offer letter and acceptance letter.

Already in your room: bed, almirah, study table, chair, and a cooler or AC as opted. Leave the furniture at home.

Other campus prohibitions
  • Alcohol, drugs, smoking or any intoxicant — zero tolerance.
  • Ragging, discrimination and sexual harassment — strictly punishable.
  • Non-vegetarian food inside hostels; the campus is vegetarian.
  • Pasting posters on walls or furniture, or burning candles and dhoop.
  • Leaving the campus without permission or a gate pass.
Hostel room handover rules
  • Report to your warden and take charge of the allotted room.
  • Check the furniture and fittings; report issues immediately.
  • Buy a lock with multiple keys and hand a spare to the warden.
  • Note the electric meter reading on the day before reporting.
  • Raise complaints through the Relationship Management System (RMS).
Before you pack

Three things freshers forget

  • A transparent document folder. Verification is much faster when originals are together.
  • Your uniform size on LPU Admit. Without it, uniform collection at Unipolis gets delayed.
  • One ethnic outfit. You will want it for the Induction Carnival and cultural events.

Know before you go

The small things that make the first fortnight feel normal.

Your ID card is your campus key

Wear it on campus. Biometrics are captured at Unipolis; the help desk tells you the collection date and venue.

UMS + LPUTouch = your daily dashboard

Timetable, attendance, results, leave requests and messages all live in UMS. Never share your login with anyone.

Homesick? You are not alone

Visit the Department of Counseling & Happiness in Block 8, Room 208, or book a counselor through UMS → Health Services.

Emergency helpline

One number for induction. One campus that has your back.

Save these before you travel. For medical emergencies call the hospital; for safety, use the 24×7 security desk.
